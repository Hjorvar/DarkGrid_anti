## 1. Overview

In Phase K of the overnight development session, the Darkgrid bestiary was expanded from 38 monsters to **68 authentic Pathfinder First Edition (1e) monsters**.

Each creature has been thoroughly researched against Paizo's official Pathfinder 1e SRD and Archives of Nethys (`aonprd.com`), ensuring:
- Accurate Pathfinder 1e HP, Armor Class, Attack Bonus, Damage Dice, Initiative, CR, and XP reward.
- Standard Pathfinder creature types (`Undead`, `Aberration`, `Humanoid`, `Animal`, `Magical Beast`, `Monstrous Humanoid`, `Plant`, `Vermin`, `Construct`).
- Full terrain distribution across all 15 Darkgrid biomes with strictly **one primary terrain** per monster.
- Support for ranged vs. airborne combat (`FLY` movement types for flying threats).
- **All 9 custom dark fantasy English descriptions** honoring Encounter Secrecy (Stage 1 never leaks the monster's identity or game stats).
- Valid 2D artwork assets on disk in both `images/monsters/` and `public/images/monsters/`.

## 3. Encounter Secrecy Audit & Verification

All 68 monsters in the Darkgrid database were verified via automated tests to satisfy:
1. `1_distant` Stage 1 text: never mentions monster name, never reveals exact creature stats.
2. `2_ranger_distant` Stage 1 Ranger text: perceptive sensory clues, zero name leaks.
3. `3_close` & `4_ranger_close` Stage 2 text: vivid standoff descriptions upon entering combat.
4. `5_health_100`, `6_health_75`, `7_health_50`, `8_health_25`, `9_dead`: progressive narrative health descriptions reflecting physical degradation instead of numbers.
5. All artwork files resolve at `images/monsters/<slug>.png` and `public/images/monsters/<slug>.png`.
6. **Artwork Audit & Approval (Post-Phase K):** All 30 monster illustrations have been created via `image-generator` in authentic 2D dark fantasy hand-painted style, inspected across 6 batches with contact sheets, verified for 100% unique SHA-256 hashes (zero Wolf placeholders), and formally **APPROVED** by the user (see `docs/monster-image-audit.md`).
