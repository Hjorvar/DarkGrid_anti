# DARKGRID ADVENTURE LAB — PHASE 2 ART BACKLOG
## Prioritized Environment Art Requirements After Phase 2 Resolver & Continuity Repairs

**Scope:** `action-ui-lab/` First-Person POV Scene Catalog (`Mode C`)  
**Policy:** In Phase 2, all misleading barrier, transition, and directional reuse mismatches were resolved using existing, geographically accurate paintings (`barrier_blackwater_bank_pov.jpg`, `barrier_darkmount_crags_pov.jpg`, `south_gate_7_8_north.jpg`, `hills_highland_ridge_pov.jpg`, `transition_plains_to_swamp_pov.jpg`) plus Tier 4 neighbor collision avoidance. No placeholder or contradictory substitute art was introduced. The remaining visual reuse occurs where a generic biome pool currently has only 2–3 distinct first-person paintings on disk.

---

## 1. Summary of Art Backlog Priorities

- **HIGH Priority (2 assets):** Dedicated paintings that eliminate the last shared landmark/generic asset (`ancient_oak_7_3_south.jpg`) and provide a dedicated southern mire transition (`HILLS → SWAMP`).
- **MEDIUM Priority (3 assets):** Fourth directional generic POV variants (`_variant_4_pov.jpg`) for `FOREST`, `SWAMP`, and `HILLS` so every pure Tier 4 interior tile can display 4 unique cardinal views (`N / E / S / W`) without sharing `EAST` and `WEST`.
- **LOW Priority (2 assets):** Additional directional variants for `PLAINS` (currently 2 generic POV variants) and `JUNGLE` (small southern cluster).
