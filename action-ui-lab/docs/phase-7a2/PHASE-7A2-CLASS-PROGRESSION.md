## 1. Progression Architecture & Milestone Philosophy

Rather than scattering dozens of micro-passives or forcing all three classes onto identical damage curves, Darkgrid's Level 1–10 progression is built around **five signature abilities per class** that deepen at clear thematic milestones:

- **Level 1 — Core Identity & Specialization:** Establishes the class's fundamental survival toolkit, equipment tradition, monster knowledge domain, and signature mechanic.
- **Level 2 — Seasoned Growth I:** Descriptive attribute growth choice (+1 to chosen attribute; retroactive HP recalculation if Constitution modifier increases).
- **Level 3 — Stronger Class Features:** First major strengthening of core combat, scouting, or recovery abilities.
- **Level 4 — Seasoned Growth II:** Descriptive attribute growth choice (+1 to chosen attribute).
- **Level 5 — Major Specialization Improvement:** Deepening of monster mastery, tactical resilience, and expedition capacity.
- **Level 6 — Seasoned Growth III:** Descriptive attribute growth choice (+1 to chosen attribute).
- **Level 7 — Improved Signature Ability:** Dramatic upgrade to the class's defining combat or survival action (*Second Wind*, *Opening Shot*, *Turn Undead* / *Divine Self-Healing*).
- **Level 8 — Seasoned Growth IV:** Descriptive attribute growth choice (+1 to chosen attribute).
- **Level 9 — Strong Thematic Mastery:** Near-apex mastery over the class's chosen domain and battlefield role.
- **Level 10 — Distinct Class Capstone & Final Growth:** Descriptive attribute growth choice (+1 to chosen attribute) plus a defining Level 10 capstone that cements the class's fantasy.

## 2. FIGHTER — THE SURVIVOR (LEVELS 1–10)

### 2.1 The Five Core Fighter Abilities

1. **Battle-Hardened** — General physical durability, bonus toughness, and resistance to shock, poison, and fatigue.
2. **Weapon Mastery** — Growing accuracy, lethality, and critical precision with all melee and ranged weapons.
3. **Know Your Enemy** — Automatic monster knowledge and offensive/defensive combat bonuses against humanoid, giant, and organized martial enemies.
4. **Armor Mastery** — Superior defensive protection when wearing armor or wielding a shield, plus reduced physical blow severity.
5. **Second Wind** — Automatic once-per-combat emergency stamina surge when reduced to critical health (`<= 30% Max HP`).

## 3. RANGER — THE HUNTER (LEVELS 1–10)

### 3.1 The Five Core Ranger Abilities

1. **Favored Enemy** — Personal hunting specialization chosen at character creation (one specific enemy category; Undead excluded). Grants scaling automatic attack, damage, and defensive instincts against that quarry.
2. **Opening Shot** — Bonus pre-combat ranged attack when the Ranger detects the monster first (`DISCOVERED`) and initiates combat with a prepared ranged weapon. Can kill the quarry before Round 1 begins.
3. **Wilderness Instinct** — Superior scouting, earlier monster detection, track reading, and protection against being ambushed (`SURPRISED`).
4. **Hunter's Knowledge** — Detailed ecological understanding of animals, magical beasts, vermin, dangerous plants, fey, dragons, and the Ranger's Favored Enemy—plus automatic combat advantages against natural wilderness creatures.
5. **Evasive Retreat** — Superior agility when disengaging (`FLEE`), increasing escape reliability and minimizing parting damage when a hunt must be abandoned.

## 4. CLERIC — THE UNDEAD HUNTER (LEVELS 1–10)

### 4.1 The Five Core Cleric Abilities

1. **Divine Self-Healing** — Primarily an **out-of-combat** sacred prayer ability that restores HP between battles, allowing longer expeditions and enabling the Cleric to begin fights at high or full health. Uses limited charges (`2` at L1, `3` at L5, `4` at L9) that recharge only when resting in a city.
2. **Undead Knowledge** — Exceptional ecclesiastical and occult knowledge of the Undead, necromancy, curses, and supernatural afflictions, unlocking full Stage 1 & Stage 2 encounter intelligence on all Undead.
3. **Sacred Strike** — Automatic consecration of the Cleric's weapon attacks against Undead, granting scaling attack accuracy, radiant/sacred damage, and bypass of undead damage resistance/incorporeal evasion.
4. **Turn Undead** — Automatic sacred disruption when confronting Undead, causing unholy creatures to hesitate, falter in accuracy, and lose supernatural momentum.
5. **Holy Protection** — Automatic ward of faith that shields the Cleric against Undead attacks, paralysis touch, energy drain, necrotic rot, and curses.

> [!NOTE]
> **Intentional General Combat Asymmetry:** Notice that the Cleric has **no** general *Weapon Mastery* bonus against ordinary living beasts or humanoids, and uses a three-quarter Base Attack progression. Fighting an Owlbear, Troll, or Orc Warchief is intentionally harder and slower for a Cleric than for a Fighter. Against the Undead, however, *Sacred Strike*, *Turn Undead*, and *Holy Protection* combine to make the Cleric the undisputed specialist.
